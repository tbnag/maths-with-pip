How to get more natural voices
  • iPad or iPhone: open Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices, then English. Download a voice marked "Enhanced" or "Premium". Then close Pip Maths and open it again.
  • Android: open Settings, then Accessibility, then Text-to-speech output, and install more voice data.
  • Windows computer: in the Edge browser, voices marked "Natural sounding" are available while the computer is online.
